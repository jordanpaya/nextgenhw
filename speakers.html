Evan Spiegel

Co-founder and CEO, Snap Inc.

Evan Spiegel started Snapchat as a Stanford student in 2011 and has run the company ever since, taking Snap public in 2017 and building it into the company behind Snapchat and Spectacles. He grew up in Los Angeles.

In 2015, at 24, he became the youngest billionaire in the world. He takes the NextGen main stage on January 23, 2027.
